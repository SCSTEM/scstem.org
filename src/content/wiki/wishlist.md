One major way you can support our teams is by donating items from this wishlist. Students use them directly in our programs and activities.

If you would like to donate any of these items, please [reach out](/contact) first so we can make sure we have the space and a current need.

## Workspace items

- Shelving
- Storage bins
- Tool and part organizers
- Lighting
- Safety equipment (safety glasses, gloves, ventilation, dust collection, etc.)
- Food and drinks during our teams' seasons (snacks, water, lunches, etc.)

## Construction materials

- Plywood, 2x4s
- Fasteners (nuts, bolts, washers, screws, etc.)
- Zip ties, tape

## Tools and equipment

- Hand tools (screwdrivers, hammers, wrenches, etc.)
- Power tools (drills, saws, etc. - especially battery powered)

## Technology

- Computers (laptops or small-form-factor desktops)
- Monitors
- Keyboards and mice

## Services

- Document printing (flyers, posters, etc.)
- Screen printing (t-shirts, banners, signage, etc.)
